## Problem statement

Campus repair activities are difficult to coordinate when requests, volunteer assignments, donated spare parts, and repair outcomes are tracked separately. This application provides one auditable database for those activities.

## Objectives

1. Let users submit and track repair requests.
2. Assign volunteers and document repair work.
3. Track donated parts from receipt to consumption without negative stock.
4. Provide role-based access and reports on repair activity and reuse.

## Scope

The system supports requesters, volunteers, and coordinators. It covers requests, assignments, sessions, parts, donations, usage, status history, and feedback. Payment, chat, and external delivery services are outside scope.

## Novelty

The application connects donated spare parts to individual campus repairs. It records how materials are reused and shows current stock, giving coordinators traceable repair and reuse data.
